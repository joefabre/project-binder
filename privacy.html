Project Binder privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026. Project Binder is made by JMTK Technology.

Short version: Project Binder does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your computer

  • Projects, documents, sources and papers you create are stored only on your device: inside the app (desktop) or in your browser's storage (web version).
  • Files you export or back up are saved where you choose.
  • JMTK Technology never receives, sees or stores any of this.

When the app uses the internet

  • Update checks (desktop app). About once a day the app asks GitHub Pages (joefabre.github.io) for a small file listing the latest version. The request reveals only what any web request does, such as your IP address to GitHub, plus the app's version and platform. You can turn this off under Settings → Updates. GitHub's privacy statement applies to its servers.
  • Links you click. "Learn more" links in the Write tab open in your web browser only when you click them.

Nothing else is sent anywhere.

Children

Project Binder collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on the Project Binder website with a new effective date.
